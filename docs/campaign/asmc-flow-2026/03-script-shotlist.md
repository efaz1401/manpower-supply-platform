## Voiceover script

### Read order and timing rule

**Write and record the Arabic first.** MSA runs roughly 10–15% longer than equivalent English. If you time the English first, the Arabic will not fit and the AR cut will feel rushed — which is the version most of your audience will watch. Time the English read *to the Arabic*, not the reverse.

Total word count is deliberately low (~135 EN words over 102s). This is documentary pacing. Silence is doing work — resist the urge to fill it.

### RTL rules for the AR cut
- Mirror all super positions horizontally. Lower-left in EN becomes lower-right in AR.
- The `1:36` stat row reverses order: EN reads `500+ · 60+ · 9` left-to-right; AR reads `٩ · +٦٠ · +٥٠٠` right-to-left.
- Use **Arabic-Indic numerals** (٤٨ / ٢٤ / ٥٠٠) in the AR cut, not Western digits. This is the detail that separates a localised film from a subtitled one.
- Any animated wipe or reveal direction must also mirror.

## Shot list — narrative beats

Full generation prompts are in `05-video-prompts.md`. This is the director's intent for each shot, so that whoever runs the prompts knows what "good" looks like and can judge a bad generation.

### Act 1 — The land

**01 · 0:00–0:06 · Aerial, LOC-DESERT, pre-dawn**
Slow high aerial drifting forward over date-palm groves and low dunes, the road cutting through. No people, no vehicles. Establishes place and scale. Cool, still, almost silent. *This is the credibility shot — if it looks generic, the film starts weak.*

**02 · 0:06–0:11 · Ground level, LOC-DESERT, dawn**
Low static shot from the road surface, warm gold rising on the horizon. A pickup passes frame left to right in the far distance — small, unremarkable, the first sign of human activity. Sunrise as the workday beginning, not as a beauty shot.

### Act 2 — The problem

**03 · 0:11–0:18 · Medium, LOC-OFFICE-CLIENT, mid-morning · FAISAL**
Faisal (thobe and ghutra, no PPE) stands over a construction drawing, hard slatted blind-light across him. He checks his watch, then looks off toward the window. Contained concern. He is not panicking — he is calculating.

**04 · 0:18–0:26 · Wide → slow push, LOC-SITE, mid-morning quiet · FAISAL**
Faisal walks into a wide shot of a site that is visibly under-staffed. Scaffolding stands idle. Two workers only, distant and out of focus. He stops and takes it in. *The emptiness is the point — resist any generation that fills the frame with activity.*

**05 · 0:26–0:31 · Close, LOC-OFFICE-CLIENT · FAISAL**
Tight on Faisal's hand picking up a desk phone, or thumb pressing a phone screen (screen dark, not visible). We do not see who he calls. Cut on the action.

### Act 3 — The process

**06 · 0:31–0:38 · Medium, LOC-OPS · KHALID**
Khalid in the ASMC ops room, phone to his ear, already writing. He nods once, decisive. The monitors behind him are dark (real dashboard composited in post). This is the tonal pivot of the film: the problem has been received by someone competent.

**07 · 0:38–0:45 · Insert, LOC-OPS · hands only**
Extreme close-up: hands laying three document folders side by side on the desk, opening one, a stamp or signature going down. No readable text. This shot carries the compliance line — Iqama, medical, insurance — visually, through nothing but the deliberateness of the hands.

**08 · 0:45–0:50 · Medium, LOC-OPS · KHALID**
Khalid stands, takes a white hard hat off the desk, and walks out of frame toward the door. Decision → movement. Cut as he clears frame.

### Act 4 — The work

**09 · 0:50–0:58 · Close, LOC-WORKSHOP · RAVI**
Ravi, welding helmet down, strikes an arc. Sparks, hard light, deep shadow. Then helmet up, and he inspects the weld he has just laid — a small nod of satisfaction. *This is the "skilled" claim, proven in eight seconds. It should be the most beautiful shot in the film.*

**10 · 0:58–1:05 · Medium tracking, LOC-SITE, busy · RAVI**
Ravi walks through a now-active site carrying equipment, tracked from the side. Background workers in hard hats and hi-vis, out of focus. Compare directly against Shot 04 — same site, transformed. That contrast is the entire argument of the film.

**11 · 1:05–1:12 · Low angle, LOC-SITE · equipment**
Low wide angle on the yellow mobile crane lifting a bundle of rebar, boom sweeping across a hard blue sky. No people in focus. The Equipment Rental pillar. Mechanical, precise, confident.

**12 · 1:12–1:19 · Two-shot, LOC-SITE · KHALID + RAVI**
Khalid and Ravi at a scaffold edge. Khalid points up at something, Ravi follows the line and nods. Khalid marks a clipboard. This is supervision, shown rather than claimed — and note that Khalid is *consulting* Ravi, not commanding him. That choice matters for how ASMC's workforce culture reads.

### Act 5 — The outcome

**13 · 1:19–1:26 · Wide crane-up, LOC-SITE, golden hour**
Camera rises from ground level to reveal the full site working in low amber light — crews on multiple levels, crane moving, long shadows. The payoff wide. This is where the `48–72 HOURS` super lands.

**14 · 1:26–1:34 · Two-shot, LOC-SITE, golden hour · KHALID + FAISAL**
Khalid (white hat) and Faisal (grey hat, thobe under vest) walk the site together, unhurried, in profile. Faisal gestures at the structure; Khalid nods. They stop. A handshake — brief, professional, not held for the camera. *No grinning. The relationship is respect, not delight.*

**15 · 1:34–1:42 · Wide static, LOC-SITE, golden hour · ALL THREE**
Khalid, Ravi and Faisal stand in a loose line facing the structure, backs three-quarter to camera, looking up at the completed floor. Low sun behind them. They are not posed and not looking at us. Hold. The stat row and end card resolve over this.
